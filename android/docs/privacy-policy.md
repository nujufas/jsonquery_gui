# jsonquery — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 2026-09-20_

jsonquery is a JSON viewer and query tool. It is designed to work entirely on
your device.

## What jsonquery collects

**Nothing.** jsonquery does not collect, store on a server, share or sell any
personal information. It has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no
tracking or crash-reporting libraries.

## Your files and data

- Files you open, text you paste or share to the app, and the results of your
  queries are processed **only on your device**. They are never uploaded.
- To open a file, jsonquery uses Android's system file picker. It only gets
  access to the files **you choose**; it does not ask for broad storage
  permissions. A copy of a file you open is kept in the app's private cache and
  is deleted the next time the app starts (or when you clear the app's storage).
- When you save results, jsonquery writes the file to the place you choose in
  the system file picker.
- If you copy something from jsonquery, or choose "Paste JSON from clipboard",
  the app reads or writes the system clipboard only in response to your action.

## Network access

jsonquery requests the Internet permission for one purpose: when you choose
**Open URL** and enter an address, the app downloads that document from the
address you typed. The request goes directly from your device to that server;
it does not pass through any server of ours. That server's own privacy policy
governs what it does with the request. The app makes no other network
requests.

## Children

jsonquery does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including
children.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at the same address
with a new date.
